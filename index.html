HARDWOOD AUCTION

A two-player NBA drafting duel. Spin the reel, stop on a legend, then out-bid your rival to build the better roster.
How it works
  • Each of you starts with 25 coins and an empty roster: PG · SG · SF · PF · C.
  • Every round the reel cycles well-known All-Stars for one position. The designated player hits STOP — it slows and lands on a name.
  • An auction opens. Bidding alternates: the stopper opens, then each bid hands the turn straight to the rival and resets the clock. Let the clock run out on your turn and you pass.
  • Pass while a bid stands and you concede — the last bidder wins, pays the coins, and locks the player into that slot.
  • If nobody bids, that player goes undrafted and the reel spins up a new one for the same slot.
  • Poach & upgrade: already own that position? Bid anyway — win and you hold both players. Keep one; the other is sold to your rival (who needs the slot) for the price it was originally won at — even if it forces them into the red. Keep the star, or sell it to drain their coins.
  • Both rosters fill 1 of each position across 10 slots. You can bid past your coins and go into debt to land a player.
  • Once you pass, you're out of that auction — no coming back in.
  • Team fit (chemistry): a balanced roster scores more. Four factors — Spacing (3P), Defense (RIM/DEF), Creation (CRT/ISO), and Chemistry (covering all of it) — each rate your five players and together boost skill by up to +25%. Draft for fit, not just OVR.
  • Final = skill × fit + coins (each reserve coin +2, each debt coin −4), then an X-Factor roll (random ±3%) so the underdog always has a puncher's chance.
